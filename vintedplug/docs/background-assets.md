# Tła do zdjęć — 24 października 2026

Dwa zasoby wygenerowano wbudowanym narzędziem image_gen (bez połączenia z API użytkownika). Zoptymalizowane pliki WebP należą do repozytorium i są serwowane z tej samej domeny. Rozmiar każdego obrazu: 1254 × 1254 px. Kompozycja nie generuje ponownie ubrania.

- Drewno: `public/backgrounds/light-oak.webp`.
- Beton: `public/backgrounds/concrete.webp`.

Prompty użyte do wygenerowania:

## Jasne drewno

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: reusable photo background texture for secondhand clothing flat lay. Create a photorealistic empty surface of light natural oak floorboards seen perfectly straight from above, orthographic view. A square image filled edge to edge with 5-6 wide vertical oak planks, subtle real wood grain and a few small understated knots, very thin natural joins. Light warm beige, neutral and softly lit uniformly across the surface. Wood should look like a clean real floor suitable behind photographed garments. No room, no walls, no horizon, no objects, no clothing, no text, no logos, no watermark, no shadows cast by external objects. Flat even exposure, no strong vignette. High resolution realistic material photograph.

## Beton

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: reusable photo background texture for secondhand clothing flat lay. Create a photorealistic empty smooth gray architectural concrete surface seen perfectly straight from above, orthographic view. A square image filled edge to edge with continuous pale neutral gray concrete, subtle irregular cement mottling and small pores, restrained realistic fine grain. Clean matte concrete suitable as a background behind clothing. Soft uniform diffuse daylight, flat even exposure, no strong directional shadows or vignette. No room, no walls, no horizon, no joints or tiles, no objects, no clothing, no text, no logos, no watermark. High resolution realistic material photograph.
